HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for a workshop?

Browse the available workshops, open the workshop you are interested in, and follow the registration instructions.

How can I view my workshop registrations?

Sign in to your account and open My Accomplishments to review your available workshop history.

What if I cannot log in to my account?

Check that your email and password are correct. If the problem continues, contact the ATHAR support team.

How can I update my profile information?

Sign in and open your account settings to review and update the profile information available to you.
